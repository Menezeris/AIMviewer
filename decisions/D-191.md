### D-191 - Orchestration model for the platform work lives in AIMforked

**Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Status:** approved (Samuel 5 Nov) · **Builds on:** D-090 (concurrent sessions), D-116 (release train), D-134 (connector routines), D-187 (CI before merge), D-189 (platform step 3)

**Context:** the orchestrator rules of October (D-090, D-116) were written for one repo and for requests from the connector. The platform plan ("Assetin platforma - stav, rozhodnutia a plán", chapter 6) runs in steps across AIMforked, AIMviewer, ifc-lite and OCB, each step in its own session started from a handoff brief. Those working rules existed only in single handoffs (`AIMforked/docs/platform/handoff-step-3.md`) and in the briefs of sessions. On 5 Nov the orchestrator was rotated for the first time (its context was nearly full); its successor needed a written model. Samuel: AIMviewer is going to merge into AIMforked, so the model lives there.

**Decision:**
1. The orchestration model is `AIMforked/docs/platform/orchestration.md` (AIMforked PR #2): roles, the source of truth, the life of a plan step from a handoff brief to a merge bundle, the handoff brief template, the checks before merge per repo, messages between sessions, orchestrator rotation and production safety. `AGENTS.md` here points to it and keeps D-090 and D-116 where it says nothing.
2. The orchestrator writes each step's handoff brief, shows it to Samuel and on his yes starts the step session itself (Samuel 5 Nov).
3. AIMforked PRs are merged by the orchestrator on Samuel's word after local checks (format, lint, typecheck, integration tests, e2e by Lukas when flows are touched); next, Actions are enabled on the fork with `lint.yml` required and `deploy-test.yml` and `release-images.yml` disabled (Samuel 5 Nov, "1+3").
4. Rotation: at about 60 % context, at the end of a merge bundle or on Samuel's word, the orchestrator posts a handoff comment on #60, its successor takes over the #60 line, the old session is archived. Done for the first time on 5 Nov (`session_016P6uF4rhtGYZdeMPmRbkyN` to `session_01RqBVf22ejDQvF4YpsCMgTk`).

**Rejected:** keeping the model in AIMviewer (it moves into AIMforked); keeping it only in the Claude Docs plan document (sessions read the repos first); Samuel pasting every step brief himself (the orchestrator has the state).

**Open:** where the D register and the orchestrator line live after AIMviewer moves into AIMforked (proposal: a pinned issue in AIMforked, #60 frozen with a pointer); whether connector requests get step-style briefs.
